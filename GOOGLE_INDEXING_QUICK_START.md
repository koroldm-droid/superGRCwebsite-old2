## 🎯 **5-Step Quick Start (10 Minutes)**

### **STEP 1: Go to Google Search Console**
🔗 https://search.google.com/search-console

**Action:**
- Click "Start Now"
- Sign in with Gmail

---

### **STEP 2: Add Your Website**
**Choose:** Domain Property

**Enter:** `supergrc.com`

**Why Domain?** Covers all versions (www, https, mobile)

---

### **STEP 3: Verify Ownership**

**Best Method: DNS (Recommended)**

1. Google shows you a TXT record:
   ```
   google-site-verification=abc123xyz456
   ```

2. Go to your domain host (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare)

3. Add DNS TXT Record:
   - **Type:** TXT
   - **Host:** `@`
   - **Value:** `google-site-verification=abc123xyz456`

4. Wait 5-10 minutes

5. Click "Verify" in Google Search Console

**Alternative: Meta Tag (Easier)**
- Get verification code from Google
- Tell me the code
- I'll add it to your site
- Deploy and verify

---

### **STEP 4: Submit Sitemap**

In Google Search Console:

1. Click **"Sitemaps"** (left sidebar)
2. Enter: `https://supergrc.com/sitemap.xml`
3. Click **"Submit"**

✅ **Done!** Google starts crawling!

---

### **STEP 5: Request Indexing**

For instant indexing:

1. Click **"URL Inspection"** (left sidebar)
2. Enter: `https://supergrc.com`
3. Click **"Request Indexing"**

✅ **Google will crawl within 24-48 hours!**
